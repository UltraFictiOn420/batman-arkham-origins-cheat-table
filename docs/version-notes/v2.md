## Hotkeys

- Main keyboard minus: Super Damage.
- Main keyboard equals: One-Hit Knockout.
- J/K/L: save / teleport / undo-redo.
- F1/F2/F3/F4: up 1/2/5/10 body heights.

Damage toggles are mutually exclusive in the table. No hotkey changes to game configuration, no console unlocker, no shared VM patches, and no popup output log.

## Switching from v1

Disable the v1 initializer, then load v2 and enable its initializer. Cheats start off. Enable options individually. Initializing/activating may briefly pause CE while it scans and verifies metadata. Reinitialize after a game restart or after switching into a mode whose metadata was unavailable.

## Caveats

Back up saves. Keep combat modifiers off for tutorials, interrogations, bosses, and other scripted encounters where finishing too quickly could disrupt progression. Boss-named classes are excluded, but this is not comprehensive proof of script safety.

Super Damage works by reducing enemy HP to one-tenth, not multiplying outgoing melee damage. Its Batarang option enables the reflected knockout permission and increases projectile damage; other gadgets are not claimed supported. Reductions to enemy HP remain after disabling until reload/respawn. Reversible knockout flags, projectile damage, gadget ranges, controller tick flags and challenge pause flags restore on disable if the same live object still exists.

Infinite Health is periodic replenishment, not invulnerability; lethal/scripted deaths and knockback remain possible. Freeze avoids disabling pawn animation ticks and only freezes eligible enemies' controllers and positions.

Infinite Upgrade Points maintains a minimum of 99 without reducing a larger balance. Granted points and purchases may persist in saves; disabling stops refill but does not undo purchases or remove granted points. It does not unlock prerequisites. Reopen the upgrade screen if its display has not refreshed.

Teleports are limited to the same pawn/world/session, have no wall/safe-ground detection and cannot stream new maps. Use L to undo and save with J in a safe location.

Challenge score changes a HUD field: medals/saved records are not verified. Timer changes are scoped to active challenge/modifier-marked HUD timers; bespoke DLC events may have different timers.
