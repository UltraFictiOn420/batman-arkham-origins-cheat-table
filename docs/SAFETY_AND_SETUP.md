# Safety and setup

Single-player Steam `BatmanOrigins.exe` only. Back up saves. Inspect embedded Lua before execution. Load only one table/version at a time, enable its required initializer, and test one feature at a time.

Disable damage/freeze/timer cheats for tutorials, scripted encounters, bosses and cutscenes until verified. Infinite health refills HP, not true hit immunity. Damage edits can persist on existing enemies after disable. Restart for a clean comparison. Upgrade points and purchases can persist in saves; disabling does not undo them.

Teleports lack collision safety and cannot guarantee loaded destinations. Save a safe position first. J/K/L save/teleport/undo; F1-F4 raise by 1/2/5/10 body heights. Main keyboard -/= toggle damage modes. No console commands needed.

Historical research helpers may WRITE another process; inspect before running. Some use installation/session-specific assumptions. Do not publish game binaries, dumps, credentials, reference tables, or savegames with this archive.
